Google's **SRE (Site Reliability Engineer)** interview process is known for being **technical, systems-focused, and reliability-driven**. It covers a blend of **software engineering, systems design, operations, and incident response**. Here's a breakdown of the types of questions you can expect:

---

## 🧠 1. **Coding Interviews**
**Format:** Typically 1-2 rounds, 45 minutes each  
**Focus:** Writing clean, correct code under time constraints

### 🧪 Sample Topics:
- Data structures: Arrays, Maps, Queues, Trees
- Algorithms: Search, Sorting, Recursion
- Real-world problem solving (e.g. log parsing, request throttling)

### ✅ Examples:
- Write a function to parse and deduplicate entries in a log file.
- Implement rate limiting for a login system.
- Find the top K frequent errors in a log stream.

> Leetcode Medium-level questions are a solid prep range (especially with a systems lens).

---

## ⚙️ 2. **Systems Design / Distributed Systems**
**Format:** Whiteboard or collaborative doc, 1 or more rounds  
**Focus:** High-level design of scalable, reliable systems

### 🔧 Example Prompts:
- Design a monitoring system like Prometheus
- Design a global cache (e.g., CDN edge cache)
- Design an alerting system with SLA guarantees
- Design a service that serves ML models at scale

**They’re looking for:**
- Awareness of tradeoffs (latency vs durability, consistency vs availability)
- Use of redundancy, fault tolerance, graceful degradation
- Monitoring, alerting, capacity planning, scaling

---

## 🚨 3. **Incident Handling / Debugging / Oncall Simulation**
**Format:** Scenario-based discussion  
**Focus:** How you handle outages, logs, alerts, escalations

### ⚠️ Sample Scenarios:
- A service has 99% latency spike — walk through diagnosing it.
- Disk space fills up on a critical node — what’s your plan?
- A region is down — how do you reroute traffic?

**What they test:**
- Calm thinking under pressure
- Root cause analysis
- Recovery strategies
- Communication & escalation

---

## 📊 4. **Linux, Networking, and Systems Knowledge**
**Format:** Quiz-style or conversational  
**Focus:** Core knowledge of OS, protocols, systems

### 🧷 Topics:
- TCP vs UDP
- DNS resolution
- Process scheduling
- Memory usage (heap vs stack)
- File descriptors, sockets
- `strace`, `tcpdump`, `top`, `lsof`, etc.

---

## 🧩 5. **Behavioral ("Googliness") / Culture Fit**
**Format:** STAR-style interview  
**Focus:** Collaboration, leadership, ownership

### 🌟 Typical Prompts:
- Tell me about a time you resolved a major outage
- A teammate pushed risky code — what do you do?
- How do you balance reliability and velocity?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Concepts to Know
- **Monitoring & Alerting:** Prometheus, Grafana, SLOs, SLIs
- **Containers & Orchestration:** Docker, Kubernetes
- **CI/CD pipelines**
- **Load balancers, reverse proxies (e.g. NGINX, Envoy)**
- **Cloud Platforms (GCP, AWS, etc.)**

---

## 🔁 Interview Format Summary:
| Round                        | Focus                        |
